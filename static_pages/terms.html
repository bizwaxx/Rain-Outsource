Terms of use

Draft dated October 21, 2026. Covers the Rain-Outsource API and MCP server at rainout-agent-source.vercel.app.

Free to use, no account

The free tier needs no account, sign-up or key. Please use it fairly. We may slow down, limit or block traffic that overloads the service or the weather services behind it, and we may change or stop endpoints. The source code is open under the MIT License, and the license file in the repository governs the code.

Odds are estimates

  • A "play probability", rain chance or "go / no-go" answer is an estimate built from forecasts and past weather. It is not a promise. Forecasts are often wrong, and fields drain differently.
  • The official status always wins. When a league, park, venue or track posts a status, that status is the answer. Check the official source before you drive out, and follow what officials on site tell you.
  • Not a safety tool. Don't use Rain-Outsource for lightning, severe weather or other safety decisions. Follow the National Weather Service, local officials and your organization's own safety rules.

Reports you send

If you send a rainout report, only report what you actually know. Keep personal information out of notes. Reports are public, unverified and deleted after 24 hours (see Privacy). We may remove reports that are false or abusive.

Data sources and attribution

Answers are built from these public sources. When you show our answers, keep the attribution lines the API returns.

  • National Weather Service / NOAA (api.weather.gov). The data is public domain. Credit: "National Weather Service (NOAA)". NWS and NOAA do not endorse Rain-Outsource, and our estimates are not official NWS products.
  • Open-Meteo (forecasts and place search). The data is licensed CC BY 4.0. Credit: "Weather data by Open-Meteo.com". The free tier uses Open-Meteo's free, non-commercial API.
  • NOAA NCEI (GHCN-Daily rainfall history), used to tune each venue's odds.
  • OpenStreetMap venue data. Credit: "Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0".
  • Official status pages from leagues, parks, venues and tracks, read as published and linked in each answer.
