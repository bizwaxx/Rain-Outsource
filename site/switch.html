Switch free in 5 minutes

Already using Rainout Line or RainedOut? Keep it. Add a free board on top that shows each field's official status and how fresh it is, the rain odds and player reports, with free calendar alerts.

1. Paste your field list
2. Get your live board and share the link
3. Add your schedule for game-by-game odds
4. Turn on free alerts

1. Add your fields

One field per line. Add the city and state if you can, or a latitude and longitude. You can also upload a CSV with a name column, plus city, state, or lat and lon columns.

Up to 25 fields. We search about 64,000 US ball and soccer fields and 162 motocross tracks (OpenStreetMap), plus 78 hand-checked venues. Fields outside the US aren't covered yet.

2. Your live board

Share this board

Post this link next to your rainout line. It always opens with these fields.

3. Import your schedule (optional)

This gives odds for every game, and a calendar your players can subscribe to with a risk note on each game.

Option A: your calendar link (best, it stays up to date)

Most league and team apps can export an iCal (.ics) or webcal link. Paste it here.

Option B: paste or upload an iCal or CSV file

CSV columns: start (like 2026-10-23T19:00:00-05:00, or separate date and time), venue, and optional title, home, away.

4. Free alerts

Free, with no per-message fee. Here's what works today and what's still coming.

Works
Calendar alerts (ICS). Import a calendar link in step 3 and you get a subscribe link. Each game shows its odds and a risk note, and your players' calendar apps refresh it on their own schedule (often every few hours). If you paste or upload a file instead, you get a one-time download that doesn't update.
Works, while this page is open
Browser notifications. Keep this page open (on a clubhouse laptop, for example) and it re-checks your board every 10 minutes. It alerts you when an official status changes, the odds drop below 50%, or a player sends a new report.
Coming soon (stub)
Web push to phones, even with the page closed. This needs a small background sender, and it isn't built yet. Nothing here signs you up.
Coming soon (stub)
Email alerts. This needs a free email-sending account, which isn't set up yet. We don't collect any email addresses on this page.

Keep your current line, add ours on top

  • Your official call stays the boss. Keep posting rainouts the way you do today. When we can read your public status page, we show your call first, and we never override it. When we can't, we say so and point players to your line.
  • Post our board link next to your line. Use the share link from step 2 on your website, in team emails, or pinned in your group chat.
  • Embed one field on your website:
<iframe src="https://rainout-agent-source.vercel.app/switch/embed?field_id=YOUR_FIELD_ID" width="340" height="230" style="border:0" title="Field status and rain odds"></iframe>
  • Players can share what they see. The "Rained out" and "We're playing" buttons on each field card send an anonymous report. Reports expire after 24 hours and never replace the official call.
  • Tell us your official status page. If your league posts status on a public page, open an issue with the link and we'll try to read it.

For AI assistants and developers

Everything on this page uses the same free API and MCP server. No key is needed. An assistant can answer "is my game on?" with the same data:

claude mcp add --transport http rain-outsource https://rainout-agent-source.vercel.app/mcp

# REST, same data as the board
GET  /v1/fields?q=Krieg&state=TX
GET  /v1/status?field_id=FIELD_ID&game_time=2026-10-23T19:00:00-05:00
GET  /v1/schedule/check?ics_url=https://…/team.ics[&format=ics]
POST /v1/schedule/check        (text/csv or text/calendar body)
POST /v1/reports  {"field_id": "…", "status": "cancelled|delayed|field_closed|playing"}

MCP tools: find_venue, get_venue_status, check_schedule, report_cancellation. More in llms.txt.
