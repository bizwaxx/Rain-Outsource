What it does

You ask a simple question, like "Is my softball game tonight still on?" or "Will it rain in Tokyo at 3 PM?" Rain-Outsource answers with a percent, one plain sentence saying why, and where the answer came from.

  • Game and race odds. It shows the official league or track status first. If there's no official word, it gives an estimate from the forecast and ten years of NOAA rain history. For dirt motocross tracks it also counts the rain from the day or two before.
  • Whole schedules. Paste a calendar link (ICS), CSV or JSON. It flags the games most likely to be rained out.
  • Rain chance anywhere. Hourly or daily rain chance and amounts for any place in the world.
  • Rainout reports. People can share "we got rained out", so everyone sees it.

What it covers today

  • About 64,000 US softball, baseball and soccer fields, plus 162 US motocross tracks, from OpenStreetMap.
  • 78 of those venues and tracks (in Texas, Oklahoma, Louisiana and Florida) are hand-checked against official rainout pages and phone lines.
  • Rain chance works worldwide. Venue odds outside the US aren't available yet; that's on the way.

It never makes up an official status. If the league hasn't said, it tells you so and gives you the rainout line to check.

When should an agent call it?

Whenever a user's plan depends on the weather: "Is the game on?", "Will the race happen Saturday?", "Which of our games this month might get rained out?", "Will it rain in London tonight?" The tools are read-only, except one that shares an anonymous rainout report, and only after the user says yes. Rain-Outsource never writes to anyone's calendar. It hands your agent a ready-made calendar event and tells it to ask the user first.

Quick start

No key, no signup. Copy and paste.

Plain web request (curl)

curl "https://rainout-agent-source.vercel.app/v1/rain?q=London&hours=6"

Claude Code

claude mcp add --transport http rain-outsource https://rainout-agent-source.vercel.app/mcp

Claude (desktop and web)

Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Paste this URL:

https://rainout-agent-source.vercel.app/mcp

Cursor, VS Code and other MCP clients (mcp.json)

{
  "mcpServers": {

ChatGPT

In ChatGPT settings, open Apps and Connectors and turn on developer mode (needs a plan that allows custom connectors). Create a connector with the MCP URL above, no authentication. For custom GPTs, import the OpenAPI file as an Action.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No key, no card, no account. Fair-use rate limits keep it up for everyone.

Does it work in my country?

Rain chance works anywhere in the world. Game and venue odds cover the US today. Worldwide venues are the goal, and we're working on it.

Is this the official word on my game?

No. The league, venue or track always has the final say, and Rain-Outsource puts their status first when it can read it. When it can't, it says so and gives the rainout line or page to check.

Can I use it for safety decisions, like lightning?

No. It isn't safety, engineering or emergency advice, and it isn't an official National Weather Service product. Follow your local weather service and your venue's safety rules.

How good are the odds?

They're an estimate, not an official call. On 154 real past events, held out, the odds pointed the right way 85% of the time. Of 39 confident "it will happen" answers (80% or more), 37 were right and 2 were wrong. How accurate is this? has the details and both misses. Always check the official status first.

Does it touch my calendar?

Never. It gives your agent a ready-made event, and your agent should ask you before adding it.

What do you log?

Only what's needed to keep it running. See the privacy page.

How do I get help or report a problem?
